Proposition 238 · A public environmental bargainWould you take the deal?

Clean power.
Wild salmon.
A hard choice.

The deal

Breach four federal dams on the lower Snake River by 2032 to give endangered wild salmon a better shot at coming back. Before any dam comes down, pay to replace what the dams do: clean electricity, grain shipping and irrigation.

$20 billion Proposed federal cap over 15 years. Roughly $60 for every American, paid once, to trade a working power system for a wild river.

We gain

About 140 miles of free-flowing river and a better chance for Snake River salmon and steelhead, every run of which is listed under the Endangered Species Act.

We give up

About 900 average megawatts of carbon-free hydropower, plus the barge route that ships grain to Lewiston, Idaho.

Who pays

Federal taxpayers fund it. Power customers, farmers, shippers and port towns carry the disruption. Tribes and fishers gain most.

The catch: nobody can promise how many fish return, or that $20 billion and a 2032 deadline will be enough.
