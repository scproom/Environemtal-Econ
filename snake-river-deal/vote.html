Official Ballot · Proposition 238

Breach Four Snake River Dams and Pay to Replace Their Benefits

Ballot Question

Shall Congress direct the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers to breach Ice Harbor, Lower Monumental, Little Goose and Lower Granite dams on the lower Snake River by December 31, 2032, and appropriate up to $20 billion over 15 years to first secure replacement carbon-free electricity, upgrade rail and highways for grain shippers, extend irrigation intakes, and compensate affected ports and communities?

Explanation. A YES vote breaches four federal dams so Snake River salmon and steelhead get back about 140 miles of free-flowing river. In exchange, the region gives up about 1,000 average megawatts of carbon-free hydropower and barge shipping for about 3.8 million tons of cargo a year. Federal taxpayers pay up to $20 billion (about $60 per American) to replace that power, move grain to rail and road, and compensate farms, ports and towns. A NO vote keeps the dams as they are.
