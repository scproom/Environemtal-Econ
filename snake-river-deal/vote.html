ECON 238 · Proposition 238

Breach Four Snake River Dams and Pay to Replace Their Benefits

Shall Congress direct the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers to breach Ice Harbor, Lower Monumental, Little Goose and Lower Granite dams on the lower Snake River by December 31, 2032, and appropriate up to $20 billion over 15 years to first secure replacement carbon-free electricity, upgrade rail and highways for grain shippers, extend irrigation intakes, and compensate affected ports and communities?

A YES vote supports the four named dam breaches, replacement services first, and up to $20 billion in federal funding over 15 years. Intended benefits include improved salmon habitat and migration conditions. Costs include lost hydropower and navigation services, replacement infrastructure and disruption to affected communities. Neither salmon recovery nor delivery within the cap and deadline is guaranteed. A NO vote rejects this package; it does not decide other salmon policies. YES, IF lets you state a condition.
