02 // THE SUBSTITUTE

A brain simulation records a real person's experience straight from their brain (everything they see, hear, smell, taste and feel, emotions included) and simulates that experience and the whole vacation in your brain, first-person.

03 // CAN WE BUILD IT?
NOT YET
1,024 electrodes vs
~1,000,000 optic nerve fibers
VR covers sight and sound. Neuralink and Synchron mostly read the brain; writing senses in is still in early trials.
BUT IT CAN
12–24 electrodes
~736,900 users
Cochlear implants restore speech with crude signals. The brain fills in gaps, so the barrier is money and trust, not physics. Path: headset, then medical implants, then full recordings.
04 // THE PAYOFF AND THE PRICE
225–900 Mt
CO₂e avoided a year if simulations replace 5–20% of leisure trips: $43–171B in avoided climate damage
37M jobs
exposed at a 10% shift. Destinations earn income back by selling their locals' recordings.
05 // WOULD YOU PLUG IN?
78%
of Americans would not want a brain chip. Their reasons: brain surgery, abandoned devices, hacking, recordings of private emotions, “meddling with nature,” and the fear of never leaving home.
SAFEGUARDS: replace extra trips, not all travel // headsets first, medical implants next // always reversible, never required
